Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Welcome to ClipFetch. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ClipFetch handles information when you use our media downloading service.

1. Information We Process

When you use ClipFetch, you may submit a media URL from a supported platform. Our backend processes that URL to retrieve available media information and prepare the requested download.

The service may also process technical information required to handle requests, diagnose errors, and maintain the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by ClipFetch is used to:

  • Validate submitted media URLs.
  • Retrieve available media information.
  • Prepare requested video or audio downloads.
  • Maintain service functionality and troubleshoot errors.

3. Temporary Files

Media files prepared for download are stored temporarily on the server. ClipFetch is designed to clean up these temporary files after the download response is completed or when a download operation fails.

Temporary file cleanup does not necessarily describe logs or records maintained by the hosting provider or other infrastructure services.

4. Third-Party Platforms

ClipFetch may communicate with third-party platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook to retrieve media information or content requested by users.

These platforms operate independently and have their own privacy policies, terms, and data-handling practices. We do not control their services.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

ClipFetch uses browser local storage to remember your preferred display theme (light or dark mode). This preference is saved using the browser's local storage.

6. Data Security

We aim to handle submitted information responsibly. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

ClipFetch is not intended to encourage children to access or download content without appropriate permission or supervision.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or its practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the ClipFetch website administrator through the contact channel provided on the website.
